    How it works

    Three questions per ticker

    Is the trend up? Price against SMA 200, and SMA 50 against SMA 200.

    Is now a good moment? RSI 14 and how far price has run above SMA 50.

    Is it leading? Its 6-month return minus the S&P 500's.

    The signals

    Strong buy

    Price above SMA 200, SMA 50 above SMA 200, RSI under 40. A pullback inside an uptrend.

    Buy

    Same uptrend, RSI under 50, or under 55 when the ticker is beating the S&P 500.

    Hold

    Same uptrend, RSI 50 to 75. Healthy trend, no discount.

    Extended

    RSI 75 or higher, or price more than 15% above SMA 50. Strong trend, bigger drops. Keep new buys small.

    Watch

    Price and SMA 50 disagree about SMA 200. Early recovery or a trend being tested.

    Selloff

    Downtrend while the S&P 500 is also below its SMA 200. Falling with the market.

    Avoid

    Downtrend while the S&P 500 is above its SMA 200. Falling on its own.

    Chart and prices

    The chart is fixed at one year of daily candles. Every indicator uses daily closes, the same basis as a standard daily chart and the GOOGLEFINANCE formulas in your spreadsheet. During market hours today's candle uses the live price, so signals settle at the 4:00 PM close.

    Where the rules come from

    • RSI: J. Welles Wilder, New Concepts in Technical Trading Systems (1978).
    • Buying low RSI only above SMA 200: Connors and Alvarez, Short Term Trading Strategies That Work (2008).
    • SMA 200 as a trend filter: Faber, "A Quantitative Approach to Tactical Asset Allocation" (2007).
    • Leaders keep leading: Jegadeesh and Titman, "Returns to Buying Winners and Selling Losers," Journal of Finance (1993).
    Prices: Yahoo Finance daily data, refreshed every 5 minutes while the app is open.
