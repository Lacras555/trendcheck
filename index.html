    How it works

    Two questions per ticker

    Is the trend up? Read from where price sits against SMA 200, and whether SMA 50 is above SMA 200.

    Is now a good moment? Read from RSI 14 and how far price has run from SMA 20 and SMA 50.

    The rules, in order

    Avoid

    Price below SMA 200 and SMA 50 below SMA 200. A low RSI here is not a bargain; downtrends stay oversold.

    Wait

    RSI 70 or higher, or price more than 15% above SMA 50. The trend may be fine but you would be chasing.

    Strong buy

    Price above SMA 200, SMA 50 above SMA 200, and RSI 40 or lower. A pullback inside an uptrend.

    Buy

    Same uptrend, RSI between 40 and 55.

    Hold

    Same uptrend, RSI between 55 and 70. Trend is healthy but there is no discount.

    Watch

    Mixed: price and SMA 50 disagree about SMA 200. Either an early recovery or a trend being tested.

    Scores

    Trend (0 to 100%): price above SMA 200 (25 pts), SMA 50 above SMA 200 (15), SMA 20 above SMA 50 (10), SMA 200 rising over 20 sessions (10).

    Entry (0 to 100%): highest for RSI 30 to 40, lower as RSI climbs, zero at 70+. Bonus for sitting within 3% of SMA 20 or 50; penalty when stretched above SMA 50.

    Where the rules come from

    • RSI and the 70/30 levels: J. Welles Wilder, New Concepts in Technical Trading Systems (1978).
    • Buying low RSI only when price is above SMA 200: Larry Connors and Cesar Alvarez, Short Term Trading Strategies That Work (2008).
    • The long moving average as a trend filter: Mebane Faber, "A Quantitative Approach to Tactical Asset Allocation," Journal of Wealth Management (2007).
    Prices come from Yahoo Finance daily closes, refreshed every 5 minutes while the market is open. SMA values match the GOOGLEFINANCE formulas in your spreadsheet (average of the last N daily closes).
